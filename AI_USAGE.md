# AI Usage — Minibus and Anbessa Bus Route Helper (Milestone 1)

## 1. AI Tools Used

- **ChatGPT:** Used to clarify Flutter concepts, explore architectural approaches, understand Riverpod, Freezed, and GoRouter, troubleshoot development issues, and improve documentation.
- **Claude:** Used for further technical explanations, discussing alternative solutions, and resolving development challenges.
- **Gemini:** Used to explore technical concepts, obtain additional explanations, and consider alternative implementation approaches.

## 2. Areas of AI Assistance

- **Application Organization:** AI tools helped explain feature-first architecture, the separation of application features, and the purpose of `lib/core/` and `lib/features/`.
- **Screen Navigation:** AI assistance was used to understand GoRouter, navigation between application screens, route configuration, and authentication-related navigation.
- **Application State and Data Handling:** AI tools helped clarify Riverpod providers, repository interfaces, in-memory data sources, and Freezed models.
- **Interface Design:** AI suggestions helped evaluate screen layouts, improve navigation consistency, and consider the overall user experience.
- **Issue Investigation and Testing:** AI tools assisted with interpreting errors, understanding test results, investigating analyzer warnings, and exploring possible fixes.
- **Project Records:** AI assistance was used to prepare and organize project documentation, including `PROPOSAL.md`, `MILESTONE.md`, and `AI_USAGE.md`.

## 3. Implementation Checks and Team Accountability

- AI tools were used to support learning, technical discussions, problem-solving, and development guidance.
- Team members remained responsible for implementation choices, reviewing changes, and completing project work.
- The application was checked using `flutter analyze --fatal-infos` and `flutter test`.
- The confirmed automated test run completed successfully with **25 passing tests**.
- Static analysis completed with **no issues found**.
- Every team member is responsible for understanding their contributions and being able to explain, test, and maintain their work during the individual oral assessment.

## 4. Principles for Using AI Responsibly

The team used AI as a supporting resource throughout development, not as a substitute for technical understanding or individual responsibility. AI assistance helped clarify unfamiliar concepts, investigate problems, compare possible solutions, and improve development decisions. The team retained responsibility for the final implementation, testing, and quality of the application.
